This short book is for researchers who work with economic text and data. Its first worked example is classifying policy sentences. The same workflow applies to literature screening, entity extraction, and document coding.

## What you will produce

By the end, you should have a task definition, a versioned annotation protocol, a review table, an error report, and a clear statement of what the resulting data can support.

The book is an initial edition. Each chapter is intentionally small enough to adapt to your own corpus and research design.
